Privacy

This site keeps nothing.

This is a personal portfolio. It collects no personal data, sets no cookies, and runs no tracking. What follows is the whole picture.

What this site does not do

  • No cookies, local storage, or any other identifier is set in your browser.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts are loaded.
  • No forms, accounts, sign-ups, or comments exist, so nothing you type is sent anywhere.
  • Every font, icon, and image is served from this domain. Opening a page makes no request to any third party.

Hosting

The site is published with GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record the IP address of visitors in its server logs for security and legal reasons. I do not have access to those logs. How GitHub handles that data is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you email me

The Contact page offers an email address and nothing else. If you write to it, I keep your message in my mailbox so I can reply, and I do not share it or add you to any list. Delivery runs through Gmail, so Google's own privacy policy covers the transport.

Links to other sites

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and the sites of the products I build lead to services with their own privacy practices. This notice covers only what happens here.

Your rights

Because this site holds no data about you, there is nothing here to access, correct, or delete. If you have a question about an email you sent me, write to the same address and I will answer. If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.
