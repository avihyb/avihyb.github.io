The Idea

Food quality is usually claimed, rarely proven. Climate Support makes it measurable: a harvest is sampled, a lab measures its nutrient density, and that result travels with the produce as a QR code anyone can scan.
Farmers earn premium pricing without organic certification. Shops source produce they can vouch for. Shoppers get proof instead of promises.

Two Products, One Chain

Landing Site climate-support.org tells the story and routes farmers, shops, shoppers, and investors to their own path, then hands partners into the platform.
Partner Platform app.climate-support.org is where the work happens: farms apply and log harvests, labs test and grade, packagers seal batches, retailers source.

How Verification Works

1. Farm A vetted farm logs a harvest and a sample is taken.
2. Lab The lab measures nutrient density and grades the batch.
3. Packager The result is sealed to a QR code printed on the packaging.
4. Shelf A shopper scans the code and checks the proof themselves.

Goals of the System

Proof a shopper can verify independently, not a label they have to trust.

A fair premium for farmers who grow nutrient-dense food, without the cost of certification.

One shared chain where farms, labs, packagers, and shops each do their own job and see the same batch.
